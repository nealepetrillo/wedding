Things to Do

The celebration wraps up at 3:00, leaving the afternoon free to explore the Finger Lakes.

MacKenzie-Childs

The whimsical pottery and home goods studio, just outside the village of Aurora.

Cayuga Lake Wineries

Dozens of wineries, cideries, and breweries line both shores of the lake.

Taughannock Falls

A short, easy walk to one of the tallest waterfalls in the Northeast, on the west shore near Ithaca.

Downtown Ithaca

Shops and restaurants on the Ithaca Commons, plus the gorges around town.

Fargo Bar & Grill

A casual village spot in Aurora for an evening drink after the festivities.

Auburn

The Harriet Tubman National Historical Park and the Seward House Museum.

Questions

What should I wear?
Late October on the lake can be crisp, so bring a layer. The ceremony is on the lawn, so block heels or flats will be happier than stilettos.
Are children welcome?
Children are welcome as long as your party fits within your allotted seat count. Please refer to your RSVP card for your seat count. If you're planning to stay overnight, note that the Inns of Aurora generally doesn't permit overnight guests under 12, so families with young children may prefer Auburn or Ithaca.
Is the venue accessible?
The ceremony is on a grass lawn and the reception is in the Lakeside Room and an adjoining tent. Let us know if you need any accommodations and we'll make sure you're comfortable.
Can I smoke or vape?
The Inns of Aurora is entirely smoke- and vape-free, including porches, patios, and lawns, and they enforce it with a fine. Please use designated areas off the property.
Will there be a bar?
Yes! Please bring ID. Guests under 40 may be asked for proof of age.
How do I RSVP?
Please return the RSVP card included with your invitation by September 28th, 2027. Lost it? Just reach out below.
